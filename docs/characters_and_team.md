# ANALOG: Characters, Skills and How the Team Forms

> Rule: every character has **one skill nobody else has**. No mission after Mission 1 can be finished by one person alone. The team only works when skills are combined.

---

## 1. The Crew

### KAEL VARN: "The Drift" (playable lead)
- **Who he is:** 29, former junior urban planner at an ORACLE contractor. Quiet, observant, dry humour he rarely uses anymore. Draws when he can't sleep.
- **Look:** cropped worn canvas jacket over his old corporate shirt, cracked temple implant with a dead cable down his neck, chest satchel with Ria's sketchbook and Dev's pencil. Hood up during heists.
- **Wound:** Ria's death, delivered as a one-line message.
- **Flaw:** won't let anyone close. Plans everything alone, which keeps failing.
- **Small habit (show, don't tell):** taps the pencil twice on the sketchbook before every big decision. Ria used to do it.
- **Unique skill: INVISIBLE TO ORACLE.** Cameras and AI systems can't predict or track him reliably. He can walk through spaces that are lethal to everyone else, as long as he doesn't act predictably.
- **Gameplay:** the only one who can enter ORACLE-monitored zones. Also the planner: sketches maps in the notebook during recon.
- **Arc:** loner → leader who learns to trust.

### MIRA OKORO: "The Ghost in the Wire"
- **Who she is:** 23, sharp, fast-talking, funny, sleeps in a van full of salvaged servers. Hides grief behind jokes.
- **Look:** oversized thrifted jacket covered in handwritten notes, fingerless gloves, one earbud always in, chipped nail polish.
- **Wound:** parents "optimized" when she was 16 for organising a protest.
- **Flaw:** reckless. Takes risks to prove she's not scared.
- **Small habit:** names every machine she hacks ("Hey Gerald, let's be friends").
- **Unique skill: SYSTEM INTRUSION.** Hacks cameras, doors, terminals, intercoms remotely, but only through a **physical connection point** someone plants for her.
- **Gameplay:** she's on comms. Kael plants a tap; Mira takes over that system.
- **Arc:** running from the truth → facing what happened to her father.

### DOC ELIAS TANE: "The Mender"
- **Who he is:** 54, former chief surgeon. Gentle, tired, deeply moral, makes terrible tea. Treats people in a flooded subway clinic.
- **Look:** long patched coat, reading glasses on a chain, steady hands, a scar across one palm.
- **Wound:** followed an ORACLE protocol that let a child die on his table.
- **Flaw:** guilt makes him freeze when lives are at stake.
- **Small habit:** counts pulses without realising it, even in conversation.
- **Unique skill: BODY AND IMPLANT CONTROL.** Can temporarily disable a person's implant (making them invisible to ORACLE like Kael), sedate guards non-lethally, fake medical emergencies, and revive an ally once per mission.
- **Gameplay:** lets *other* people enter ORACLE zones by masking their implants for a short time. Enables mercy routes.
- **Arc:** guilt → choosing to act instead of obeying.

### JUN REYES: "The Wall"
- **Who he is:** 38, ex-ORACLE enforcer. Huge, calm, speaks very little. Feeds stray cats.
- **Look:** heavy work jacket with the enforcer badge torn off, cybernetic right arm with scratched paint, buzzcut, tired eyes.
- **Wound:** carried out "optimizations" for 12 years. Believed he was keeping people safe.
- **Flaw:** thinks he deserves to suffer; volunteers for suicide roles.
- **Small habit:** always sits facing the door.
- **Unique skill: STRENGTH AND SECURITY KNOWLEDGE.** Lifts and holds heavy objects (shutters, crates, people), knows guard protocols and patrol routines, can bluff as an enforcer.
- **Gameplay:** opens physical routes nobody else can, holds a door while the team gets through, predicts guard behaviour.
- **Arc:** atonement through self-punishment → atonement through protecting others.

### SERA LIND: "The Architect"
- **Who she is:** 34, senior ORACLE engineer. Brilliant, precise, polite, lonely. Lives in a perfect apartment she hates.
- **Look:** immaculate grey corporate suit, small ORACLE pin, hair always tied, but chewed fingernails.
- **Wound:** helped build ORACLE believing it would end suffering.
- **Flaw:** lies as a survival reflex, even to people she trusts.
- **Small habit:** straightens objects when nervous.
- **Unique skill: INSIDE ACCESS.** Knows ORACLE's architecture. Can create blind spots in its predictions, grant credentials, and warn the team what ORACLE is about to do next.
- **Gameplay:** reveals ORACLE's "prediction" of your plan so you can deliberately do something else. Only she can open the final core.
- **Arc:** hiding → confessing the worst thing she's done.

## 2. How Skills Combine (the team "equation")

| Combo | Result |
|---|---|
| **Kael + Mira** | Kael walks unseen into a monitored room and plants a tap; Mira takes over the building |
| **Doc + Jun** | Doc masks Jun's implant; Jun walks into an ORACLE zone and carries out a patient/prisoner |
| **Mira + Sera** | Sera shows which system ORACLE watches least; Mira hacks it without triggering alarms |
| **Jun + Kael** | Jun holds a shutter or distracts guards with a fake enforcer order while Kael slips through |
| **Doc + Kael** | Doc fakes a medical emergency to empty a floor; Kael moves while staff are distracted |
| **Sera + Kael** | Sera leaks ORACLE's predicted route for the team; Kael picks the unpredicted one |
| **All five** | The Convergence finale: each skill opens one layer of ORACLE's core |

**Design rule:** if an ally is lost (betrayal, death, low trust), the player must find a harder workaround for that skill. Never a dead end, always a cost.

## 3. How They Meet (each first meeting is a mission)

| Order | Character | How they bump into each other |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mira** | During *Ghost Record*, Kael breaks into the records office. Mira is already in the system trying to find her parents' files. She sees an "unpredictable" signal on the cameras, which is Kael, and hijacks his earpiece: "Whoever you are, you're about to walk into a guard. Turn left." They need each other to get out. |
| 2 | **Doc Tane** | Kael is injured escaping Mission 1. Mira takes him to the only doctor who won't report him. Doc patches him up, then asks for a favour: a child patient has been taken by ORACLE's hospital. |
| 3 | **Jun** | Jun is the enforcer guarding the hospital in Mission 2. He catches Kael, and recognises his surname. He lets them go, quietly. Days later he turns up at Doc's clinic, bleeding, having deserted: "You'll need someone who knows how they think." |
| 4 | **Sera** | The team keeps finding anonymous help: unlocked doors, warnings. In Mission 3 they trace it to Sera. She has been watching Kael because ORACLE flagged him as "anomalous" and she wants to know why. She asks to be extracted. |

**Key idea:** every member joins because the previous mission went wrong and they needed someone new. The team grows from need into family.

## 4. Mission Path to the Grand Finale

| # | Mission | Team | New skill unlocked | Story step | Hidden choice |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ghost Record** | Kael + Mira (comms) | Kael's invisibility + Mira's hacking | Kael erases his file and finds Ria's: `Status: reallocated` | Spare or silence the night clerk |
| 2 | **The Medic's Debt** | Kael, Mira, Doc | Doc's implant masking | Rescue the child from the hospital; meet Jun as an enemy | Save the child's mother too (risky) or just the child |
| 3 | **Blackout** | Kael, Mira, Doc, Jun | Jun's strength and guard knowledge | Cut a district off ORACLE; find the anonymous helper is Sera | Full blackout (civilians suffer) or slower targeted route |
| 4 | **The Informant** | Full team of 5 | Sera's inside access | Extract Sera from the corporate tower; Null hunts them for the first time | Trust Sera's plan or Kael's own plan |
| 5 | **Vault 9** | Full team | All combos required | Break into ORACLE's archive; learn Ria's mind is inside ORACLE and Sera wrote the algorithm. Betrayal check fires here | Tell the team the truth or hide it; forgive, confront or exile Sera |
| 6 | **Convergence** | Whoever is left + Anya | Every skill opens a layer of the core | Rescue Anya, face Vance and Null, meet Ria's echo | The final choice decides the ending |

### Safe-house interludes (between every mission)
Quiet scenes where trust is built: cooking, fixing gear, Mira teaching Jun a card game, Doc stitching Kael's hand, Sera standing at the window not sleeping. Optional conversations, side jobs, and small gifts (a sketch, a meal, keeping a secret) shift trust. No combat, no timer.

### Personal side missions (unlocked at Bonded trust)
- **Mira:** find where her father was sent.
- **Doc:** face the family of the child he lost.
- **Jun:** return to someone he once optimized and ask for forgiveness.
- **Sera:** delete her own name from ORACLE's honours wall, and decide whether to confess.

---

## 5. Why this makes people cry
- The team starts as strangers who use each other and ends as a family.
- Everyone is partly guilty, so forgiveness means something.
- The skills the player relied on for 10 hours become literal sacrifices in the finale. If Jun holds the last door, the player feels it.
- Kael, who couldn't trust anyone, ends up trusting four people with his life, and the one person he couldn't save is waiting at the end.
