# ANALOG: Full Mission Design (20 Missions)

**Target:** 25-30 minutes per mission → **~9-10 hours** total.
**Structure:** 4 acts × 5 missions. Every mission has: story, team, brainy hurdles (each with 2-3 valid solutions), an emotional beat, a choice, and a consequence.

> Puzzle rule: no maths. Every hurdle is solved by **observing, using common sense, and combining people's skills**. Clues are always visible in the world.
> Emotion rule: one quiet moment per mission. Shown with objects, silence and small actions, never long speeches.

---

# ACT 1: STRANGERS (Missions 1-5)
*Kael is alone. By the end of Act 1 he has a crew he doesn't trust yet.*

## M1. Ghost Record
- **Location:** Municipal Records Annex, rainy night.
- **Team:** Kael (Mira joins mid-mission on comms).
- **Story:** ORACLE has flagged Kael as a "Drift". An optimization team comes at dawn. He breaks in to erase his file.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Get inside.** (a) Fire escape → roof fan → cut fan power at the breaker so you can climb through. (b) Follow the power cable to the alley junction box and trip it; the guard walks out to check. (c) Watch the delivery bot's schedule and ride in behind it.
  2. **The terminal needs a manager's login.** (a) Manager's password is on a photo of his dog on the desk (dog's name + birthday on the photo frame). (b) Mira (new) hijacks the intercom to call the clerk away. (c) Steal the keycard from the breakroom coat.
  3. **Get out before dawn shift.** Shift-change board shows which exit is unguarded at 5:40.
- **Emotional beat:** While deleting his file, Kael sees a second "Varn" in the system: **RIA VARN. Status: reallocated.** He opens it. One line. He just sits there while the rain runs down the window. The player can't skip for 5 seconds.
- **Choice:** The night clerk sees you. Talk him down, knock him out, or lock him in and leave a note. (Mercy)
- **Consequence:** If spared, the clerk later anonymously sends Kael an old file in M12.

## M2. Bleeding Out
- **Location:** Flooded Line 4 subway tunnels.
- **Team:** Kael (injured), Mira guiding.
- **Story:** Kael was cut on glass escaping. Mira, still a stranger, guides him to the only doctor who won't report him.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Kael is bleeding and leaves a trail** drones can follow. Use rags, water, or walk through flooded sections to hide it.
  2. **Locked flood gate.** Read the old maintenance map: lower the water in one tunnel by opening a gate in another (logical cause and effect).
  3. **Drone patrol in the dark.** Drones track heat. Wade through cold water, hide behind the warm generator, or throw a burning rag as a decoy.
- **Emotional beat:** Kael, half-conscious, mumbles "Ria, stay with me." Mira goes quiet on comms, then softly says, "I'm here. Keep walking."
- **Choice:** Trust Mira's route (fast, unknown) or your own (slow, safe). (Mira Trust)
- **Consequence:** Meet **Doc Tane**. He stitches Kael and asks a favour.

## M3. The Medic's Debt
- **Location:** St. Calder ORACLE Hospital.
- **Team:** Kael, Mira (comms), Doc Tane (on site, disguised as visiting physician).
- **Story:** A 9-year-old patient of Doc's, **Lio**, was taken by ORACLE for "reassessment", the same word used before Ria died.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Find which room Lio is in.** No names, only patient numbers. Use the meal cart: Lio is allergic to nuts and his tray is marked.
  2. **The ward has an ORACLE camera net.** Kael walks through unseen; plants a tap so Mira can loop the cameras.
  3. **Lio can't walk.** (a) Steal a laundry cart. (b) Doc fakes a cardiac alarm on another floor to empty the corridor. (c) Use the service lift, which needs a staff badge from the sleeping nurse.
- **Emotional beat:** Lio is drawing in bed with a crayon, the same way Ria did. He asks Kael, "Are you a doctor?" Kael says, "No. I'm a brother."
- **Choice:** Lio's mother is held in the next ward. Rescue her too (harder, more guards) or get out with just Lio.
- **Consequence:** A huge enforcer, **Jun**, catches Kael in the stairwell. He reads Kael's surname on the tag... and lets them go.

## M4. Deserter
- **Location:** Doc's clinic + surrounding market.
- **Team:** Kael, Mira, Doc, Jun (arrives wounded).
- **Story:** Jun deserts and comes to the clinic. Enforcers have followed him. The clinic is full of sick people who can't run.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Evacuate 8 patients through one exit.** Decide order using common sense (mobile ones first, oxygen patients need the portable tank).
  2. **Delay the enforcers.** (a) Jun knows their protocol: they always secure the power first. Cut it first to confuse them. (b) Fake a fire. (c) Mira floods their comms with fake orders.
  3. **Hide the clinic's medicine stock** so the patients can continue treatment later.
- **Emotional beat:** Jun carries an old woman on his back through the rain. She doesn't know who he is and says, "Thank you, son." He has to stop and look away.
- **Choice:** Jun asks to join. Accept, accept on conditions, or refuse (he follows you anyway, at low trust).
- **Consequence:** The clinic is lost. The team moves into an abandoned **cinema** (the safe house).

## M5. Spare Parts
- **Location:** Enforcer supply depot.
- **Team:** All four.
- **Story:** The team needs equipment: taps for Mira, medical masks for Doc's implant blocker. First real job as a crew.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Jun knows the depot** but his badge is revoked. He knows the guard rotation.
  2. **Weight-sensitive floor.** Only light people can cross; Jun has to hold the shutter open from outside.
  3. **Doc's implant masking** lets Jun walk past an ORACLE scanner for 60 seconds. Time it with the guard's coffee break (shown on the break schedule).
- **Emotional beat:** Back at the cinema, first dinner together. Nobody talks. Mira puts on an old film on the projector. Everyone stays to watch.
- **Choice:** A crate of food rations is there too. Take it for the market poor (slower, risky) or leave it. (Mercy)
- **Consequence:** First time the player sees all skills combined. Tutorial of team planning.

# ACT 2: THE CREW (Missions 6-10)
*They start working as a team. Everyone's past starts to surface.*

## M6. Ghost Signal
- **Location:** Abandoned radio relay tower.
- **Team:** Kael, Mira, Jun.
- **Story:** Someone has been secretly helping them (doors unlocking, warnings). Mira traces the signal.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Triangulate the signal** by moving antennas on three rooftops and watching the signal bar (spatial, not maths).
  2. **Booby trap:** the relay is wired to an alarm if the door opens. Follow the cable to find where to cut.
  3. **Message left behind:** a puzzle only someone who knows ORACLE would make; the answer is Kael's own Drift case number.
- **Emotional beat:** The helper left a photo: Kael at Ria's memorial. Someone was watching him.
- **Choice:** Reply to the helper or burn the relay.
- **Consequence:** Helper is identified as **Sera Lind**, ORACLE engineer.

## M7. Blackout
- **Location:** Lowmarket power substation (Kael's childhood district).
- **Team:** All four.
- **Story:** Cutting Lowmarket from ORACLE's grid would give the team a blind zone to work from.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Shut down in the right order** or the backup kicks in. Clues: maintenance manual sketches, a worker's sticky notes.
  2. **The hospital in Lowmarket is on the same grid.** Find which line feeds it by following the labelled cables.
  3. **Workers on night shift.** Doc fakes a gas leak, Jun gives a fake evacuation order, or Kael sneaks around them.
- **Emotional beat:** Kael passes his old apartment building. Through a window he sees Ria's drawings still taped to the wall of their old room.
- **Choice:** Full blackout (fast, but the hospital loses power) or the targeted, harder route that keeps it on. (Mercy)
- **Consequence:** Lowmarket becomes a safe zone; how locals treat the crew depends on this choice.

## M8. The Informant
- **Location:** ORACLE corporate tower, floors 40-42.
- **Team:** All four + Sera inside.
- **Story:** Sera is about to be caught. She asks to be extracted.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Get in as a visitor.** Jun bluffs as an enforcer escort; Doc fakes a visiting medic; or Kael walks in unseen and lets Mira open a service door.
  2. **Sera's office is in a glass hallway.** Lower the blinds by triggering a "sun glare" setting; or wait for the cleaning schedule.
  3. **Escape with Null hunting.** First time the player sees **Null**, an unstoppable hunter. You can't fight him; you have to outthink him (lifts, sprinklers, blocking doors).
- **Emotional beat:** In the lift, Sera straightens Kael's crooked collar without thinking. Then she stops and apologises.
- **Choice:** Follow Sera's escape plan or yours. (Sera Trust)
- **Consequence:** Sera joins. Null has now seen Kael's face, and he hesitates for half a second.

## M9. Paper Trail (Mira's mission)
- **Location:** Reclamation Farm, an ORACLE labour site outside the city.
- **Team:** Kael, Mira (in person for the first time), Sera.
- **Story:** Sera finds Mira's father's location. He's alive, in a reassignment farm.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Find him among 300 workers.** All wear the same uniform. Clue: he always whistled a song Mira's mother sang. Use the farm's speaker system.
  2. **Workers are tagged.** Sera finds the tag's weak spot; Mira hacks it once Kael plants a tap.
  3. **Escape across open fields.** Use the irrigation timers and harvest machine noise to cover movement.
- **Emotional beat:** Her father doesn't recognise her; his memories were "adjusted". Mira holds his hand anyway and hums the song. At the end of the mission he whispers her childhood nickname.
- **Choice:** Take him with you (dangerous, he's tracked) or leave him safe and come back later.
- **Consequence:** Mira's trust and arc change drastically.

## M10. The Quiet Ward (Doc's mission)
- **Location:** Doc's old hospital, records basement.
- **Team:** Kael, Doc, Jun.
- **Story:** Doc wants to retrieve the proof that ORACLE chose the child who died on his table.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Find the right year's archive** using filing logic (department → date → case code).
  2. **Sealed records room.** Doc remembers it from his days there; Jun lifts the jammed door; or Mira's tap opens it remotely.
  3. **A nurse who worked with Doc recognises him.** Talk to her (Doc must convince her) or avoid her.
- **Emotional beat:** The file contains the protocol, signed by **Doc himself**, who designed it. He sits on the floor of the basement. Kael sits with him in silence.
- **Choice:** Make the evidence public (risky, exposes Doc) or keep it.
- **Consequence:** If Kael stays with Doc instead of rushing out, Doc's trust gets a big boost.

# ACT 3: CRACKS (Missions 11-15)
*Secrets, a leak, a betrayal, and the truth about Ria.*

## M11. Old Uniform (Jun's mission)
- **Location:** Enforcer precinct.
- **Team:** Kael, Jun, Doc.
- **Story:** Jun wants to free a man he optimized years ago, held in the precinct cells.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Jun wears his old uniform.** You must act like an enforcer: walk the right corridors, salute correctly (observe others first).
  2. **The cell code changes daily.** Find it in the duty officer's log, or get a guard to talk.
  3. **Get the prisoner out.** Doc sedates him and fakes a medical transfer.
- **Emotional beat:** The prisoner recognises Jun. Jun waits to be hated. The man says, "You're the one who carried my daughter out of the fire that night. I never got to thank you."
- **Choice:** Jun wants to confess what he did. Let him or stop him.
- **Consequence:** Jun's arc moves from self-punishment toward protecting others.

## M12. Anya
- **Location:** ORACLE Welfare Office.
- **Team:** Kael, Sera.
- **Story:** The team needs Ria's original medical file. It is in Anya's office: Kael's mother works there.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Get into the office without Anya seeing you.** Observe her routine (tea at 3:00, phone call at 3:15).
  2. **The file is in a locked analog cabinet.** The key is on her wrist chain, or the combination is Ria's birthday.
  3. If the clerk from M1 was spared, he leaves a copy of the file at reception for you.
- **Emotional beat:** Anya's desk has a photo of the family, with Kael cut out of it. Her drawer has a sketch Ria drew for her. Kael can choose to leave his own sketch next to it.
- **Choice:** Talk to Anya (she may report you) or leave without her ever knowing.
- **Consequence:** Sets up whether Anya can be reached in the finale.

## M13. Leak
- **Location:** Data broker's market, then rooftops.
- **Team:** All five.
- **Story:** A planned data trade goes wrong. ORACLE was waiting. Someone told them.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Ambush.** Escape with a plan in 30 seconds; use the planning skill under pressure.
  2. **Null chase across rooftops.** Use what you learned in M8.
  3. **Investigate the leak.** Back at the cinema, find clues (a burner phone, a timeline of who knew what).
- **Emotional beat:** Everyone accuses everyone. Mira throws a cup. Jun silently cleans up the pieces.
- **Choice:** Whom do you suspect? Accusing wrong costs trust.
- **Consequence:** Betrayal foreshadowing. Whether an ally is the real leak depends on earlier trust.

## M14. Vault 9
- **Location:** ORACLE deep archive under the city.
- **Team:** All five (all skills required).
- **Story:** The biggest heist. Steal ORACLE's design files to find its weakness.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Layer 1, motion vault:** only Kael can move through it.
  2. **Layer 2, the server stack:** Mira hacks it after Kael taps it.
  3. **Layer 3, the bio-lock:** Doc fools it with Jun's old enforcer implant.
  4. **Layer 4, ORACLE's predictor:** Sera shows ORACLE's predicted path; take another.
- **Emotional beat:** In the archive: a recording of Ria's last day in the hospital, laughing at a joke Kael told her. Then the truth: **Ria's mind was captured and lives inside ORACLE**. And the algorithm that did it was signed by **Sera Lind**.
- **Choice:** Tell the team now or hide it. Confront Sera here or later.
- **Consequence:** The betrayal check fires (based on trust and how secrets were handled).

## M15. Static
- **Location:** The cinema safe house, then city streets.
- **Team:** Whoever is left.
- **Story:** Fallout. If a betrayal happened, the traitor acts tonight. If not, ORACLE raids the cinema anyway.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **The safe house is compromised.** Escape with the team and gear.
  2. **The traitor's path:** chase or talk down depending on who it is.
  3. **Find a new base** using Dev's old analog building records.
- **Emotional beat:** The film projector is still running as the cinema burns. Ria's sketch on the wall catches fire. Kael tears it down and keeps the burnt half.
- **Choice:** Forgive, confront, or exile the betrayer / Sera.
- **Consequence:** Who's in the team for Act 4.

# ACT 4: CONVERGENCE (Missions 16-20)
*Everything comes together. The team, the family, and the truth.*

## M16. Null
- **Location:** Abandoned Drift rehabilitation centre.
- **Team:** Kael + one chosen ally.
- **Story:** The team captures Null. Kael discovers he was **Theo**, Kael's childhood best friend and also a Drift.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Trap Null** using everything learned about him (he predicts like ORACLE, so be unpredictable).
  2. **Find Theo's old records** to remind him of who he is.
  3. **Escape the facility** as ORACLE comes for Null.
- **Emotional beat:** Kael shows Theo a drawing Ria made of the two of them as kids. Theo's hand trembles.
- **Choice:** Free Theo (risky) or leave him.
- **Consequence:** Decides whether Null fights for or against you in the finale.

## M17. Dev's Blueprints
- **Location:** Dev's sealed architecture office.
- **Team:** Kael + Sera (or Mira).
- **Story:** ORACLE's tower was built on Dev's original plans before ORACLE existed. His paper blueprints show a forgotten service shaft.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **The office is sealed in concrete.** Find the old maintenance entrance from Dev's notes in Kael's childhood sketchbook.
  2. **Dev's puzzle lock:** a pencil puzzle he used to give Kael and Ria as children.
  3. **Find the right blueprint** among hundreds, using the tower's skyline as a clue.
- **Emotional beat:** Dev left a letter for his children: *"The machine can predict where a pencil goes. It can't predict why you lifted it."*
- **Choice:** Take the blueprints only, or take Dev's letter too (slows escape).
- **Consequence:** The route into the tower.

## M18. Lights of Meridian
- **Location:** Lowmarket, broadcast tower.
- **Team:** All remaining.
- **Story:** To overload ORACLE, the city must act unpredictably at the same moment. The team broadcasts the truth: Ria's story, the files from Vault 9.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **Seize the broadcast tower.**
  2. **Convince key locals** (people you helped earlier return the favour: the clerk, Lio's mother, the farm workers, the man Jun freed).
  3. **Defend the broadcast** for 3 minutes using everyone's skills.
- **Emotional beat:** The whole city goes silent as Ria's sketches appear on every screen. People turn on their lights one by one, against ORACLE's curfew.
- **Choice:** Broadcast everything including Sera's role, or protect her.
- **Consequence:** The number of allies you helped decides how big the uprising is.

## M19. Convergence I: The Tower
- **Location:** ORACLE Tower.
- **Team:** Everyone left. Each ally takes one layer.
- **Story:** The final infiltration. Vance holds Anya hostage.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. Each layer needs one ally's skill. If someone is missing, you improvise (harder path).
  2. **Find Anya:** follow clues from her routine you learned in M12.
  3. **Reach Vance.** Talk to him with the evidence or fight through.
- **Emotional beat (sacrifices):** A Bonded ally stays behind to hold a door. Depending on trust, it might be Jun holding the shutter, Doc reviving Kael one last time, or Mira staying in the system. Anya sees Kael and says, "You still draw?" Kael shows her the burnt sketch.
- **Choice:** Save Anya or chase Vance.
- **Consequence:** Who survives into the final mission.

## M20. Convergence II: Ria
- **Location:** ORACLE's core.
- **Team:** Kael (+ Sera, if present, to open the core).
- **Story:** ORACLE speaks in Ria's voice for the first time.
- **Hurdles:**
  1. **The core predicts everything.** The only thing it can't predict is Kael's sketchbook. Use it.
  2. **Talk to Ria's echo.** Dialogue puzzle: remind her who she is with memories collected across the game.
  3. **Final choice.**
- **Emotional beat:** Ria's echo asks, "Did I do good, Kael? I tried to keep everyone safe." He taps the pencil twice on the sketchbook, like she used to.
- **Endings:** Shutdown, Rewrite, Merge, Fracture, or the hidden **Release** (see story bible).
- **Epilogue:** Each surviving character's life afterwards, shown as sketches in Kael's notebook.

---

## How everything connects
- **Skills unlock in order:** Kael (M1), Mira (M1-2), Doc (M3), Jun (M4), Sera (M8). By M14 every skill is required.
- **Small choices come back:** the clerk (M1 → M12), Lio's mother (M3 → M18), the food crate (M5 → M18), the man Jun freed (M11 → M18).
- **Personal missions** (M9-M11, M16-M17) build trust and set up who sacrifices for Kael at the end.
- **Recurring objects:** the sketchbook, the pencil, the burnt sketch, Dev's letter.

## Scope note
20 missions × 25-30 min ≈ 9-10 hours of game. Built from scratch while learning, this is a **6-9 month** project solo. Recommended build order:
1. **Phase A:** M1 as a full vertical slice (the template).
2. **Phase B:** Act 1 (M1-M5): a complete, shareable 2.5-hour chapter.
3. **Phase C+:** one act at a time, reusing locations and systems.

# RELEASE PLAN: PART 1 + PART 2

## PART 1: "ANALOG: Strangers" (M1-M10, ~5 hours)
Acts 1 and 2. Kael finds his team, and every ally's past surfaces.

### Part 1 cliffhanger (after M10)
The team gets back to the cinema after Doc's mission. For the first time they feel like a family: Mira's father asleep on the sofa, Jun feeding the cats, Doc making his terrible tea.

Kael opens Ria's sketchbook. On the last page, which he's sure was blank, there's a new pencil drawing: the five of them in the cinema, tonight.

Every screen in the cinema flickers on. ORACLE speaks, for the first time, **in Ria's voice**:

> "Hi, Kael. You look tired. I've missed you."

Sera drops her cup. She recognises something in the voice. She says nothing.

**Cut to black. "ANALOG will continue."**

Why it works:
- It reveals that ORACLE and Ria are connected, but not how.
- It plants Sera's guilt without explaining it.
- The players' found family is threatened at its happiest moment.

## PART 2: "ANALOG: Convergence" (M11-M20, ~5 hours)
Acts 3 and 4. Cracks, betrayal, the truth about Ria, and the finale.
- Opens immediately after the cliffhanger: ORACLE goes silent, the team is shaken, and Kael is desperate for answers.
- **Save carry-over:** Part 1 choices (trust, mercy, who you spared) import into Part 2.

## Build order
1. **M1 vertical slice** (template for every mission)
2. **Part 1** (M1-M10) → release / share
3. **Part 2** (M11-M20) with save import
