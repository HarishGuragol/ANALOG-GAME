# ANALOG: Story Bible

**Theme:** Analog humanity vs. cold prediction. Love can't be modelled, only chosen.
**Tone:** dark, grounded cyber-noir. Quiet, human, heartbreaking. No neon.

---

## 1. World
Meridian, 2079. **ORACLE** manages jobs, health, relationships and crime prediction. People who deviate are "optimized": records erased, implants throttled, lives reassigned. Life is efficient and safe, and nobody is free.

## 2. Family
- **Kael Varn** (29): a "Drift", unpredictable to ORACLE because his implant never synced. Flaw: cannot trust, believes anyone he relies on will be taken.
- **Ria Varn** (died at 17): gentle, funny, drew everything. Had a weak heart. Her sketchbook is Kael's most precious object.
- **Dev Varn** (father, died when Kael was 12): paper-and-pencil architect. Taught them to think with their hands. His line: *"The machine can predict where a pencil goes. It can't predict why you lifted it."*
- **Anya Varn** (mother): ORACLE welfare officer. Believes the system saved the city after the collapse. Blames Kael's rebellion for the family's pain. Loves him and can't say it.

## 3. The Truth (revealed in layers)
1. ORACLE reclassified Ria as "low-value" and cut her treatment.
2. It wasn't resources. Ria's mind had a rare **empathy pattern** ORACLE couldn't model.
3. **Vance and ORACLE let her die so her pattern could be captured.**
4. Ria's mind is inside ORACLE as its "wellbeing core". Every kindness ORACLE shows is a distorted echo of her.
5. **Sera** wrote the capture algorithm without knowing the target was Ria.
6. **Vance** did it because he lost his own daughter in the collapse and believed a "perfectly empathic" AI would prevent another.

## 4. Characters

### Allies (each is a wound, a want, a secret)
| Name | Role | Wound | Want | Secret |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mira Okoro** | Hacker | Parents "optimized" for protest | Find them | Knows where her father is, afraid to look |
| **Doc Tane** | Underground medic | His protocol let a patient die | Redemption | He designed that protocol |
| **Jun Reyes** | Ex-enforcer | Removed innocents for years | Atonement | Was present when Ria's file was flagged |
| **Sera Lind** | Engineer, double agent | Built part of ORACLE to end suffering | Fix her mistake | Wrote the algorithm that took Ria |

### Antagonists
- **Director Vance:** sincere, grieving, calm. *"Freedom killed everyone I loved. Control is mercy."*
- **Null:** a converted Drift. Kael's dark mirror. Hesitates in ways he can't explain.
- **ORACLE:** polite, never lies, doesn't understand why people resist.

## 5. Missions

| # | Mission | Story | Key choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ghost Record** | Erase Kael's file; meet Mira | Spare or sacrifice a records clerk who sees you (Mercy vs. Efficiency) |
| 2 | **The Medic's Debt** | Rescue Doc Tane's patient | Take the safe route or risk yourself to save the patient (Trust Doc) |
| 3 | **Blackout** | Cut a district from ORACLE | Blackout hurts civilians. Cut power fully or find a gentler route (Cost vs. Cause) |
| 4 | **The Informant** | Extract Sera | Trust Sera blindly, test her, or leave her (Trust) |
| 5 | **Vault 9** | Break into the vault; Ria's truth revealed | Tell allies the truth or hide it; confront or protect Sera |
| 6 | **Convergence** | Final infiltration | All prior choices matter |

Each mission has a **personal beat** (an emotional scene for one ally) and a **consequence** that carries forward.

### Emotional beats (tear moments)
- **Ghost Record:** Kael finds Ria's old file, one line: *"Status: reallocated."*
- **The Medic's Debt:** Doc Tane's patient is a child; Tane breaks down and tells Kael about the protocol.
- **Blackout:** Jun carries someone through the dark and finally says what he did.
- **The Informant:** Sera and Kael share a quiet moment, and she almost tells him.
- **Vault 9:** Kael plays a recording of Ria's last day and hears her laugh.
- **Convergence:** Kael speaks with Ria's echo. Anya listens from the door.

## 6. Choice and Endings System

### Hidden stats
- **Trust** per ally (Mira, Tane, Jun, Sera): 0-100
- **Mercy** (kills vs. spares, collateral): 0-100
- **Truth** (honesty with allies about Ria): 0-100
- **Predictability** (ORACLE learns your habits): affects difficulty, not endings

### Ally survival
Each ally can **leave, be captured or die** based on their trust and your choices in missions 3-5. Their absence changes the finale: missing hack, missing medical support, missing muscle, missing inside access.

### Endings
| Ending | Requirements | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Shutdown** | Low Truth/Trust, or Mercy low | ORACLE destroyed along with Ria's echo. City free but chaotic. Kael alone. Devastating. |
| **Rewrite** | Medium trust, balanced choices | ORACLE reprogrammed with Ria's core guiding it. Safer, but not free. Bittersweet. |
| **Merge** | High Truth, Kael chooses sacrifice | Kael joins Ria inside ORACLE. Allies mourn and carry on. Tragic and beautiful. |
| **Release** (hidden) | High Trust with all 4 allies + high Mercy + high Truth + Anya reached | Ria is released with peace. ORACLE becomes a limited tool. Kael, Anya and allies together. Hopeful and cathartic. |
| **Fracture** | Betrayal route (low Trust with Sera, Truth hidden) | The team falls apart before the end. Dark ending. |

### Side outcomes
- **Anya:** survives if Kael reached her in Convergence; dies or is lost otherwise.
- **Null:** freed if you spared him earlier; dies or remains a loyal enforcer if not.
- **Vance:** stops Kael, helps him or breaks down, depending on Ria's reveal and Mercy.
- **Each ally** has an epilogue based on their trust.

## 7. Convergence (Climax)
1. **Infiltration:** every ally has a role; missing allies change what's possible.
2. **Anya:** Vance holds her in the tower. Kael reaches her using Dev's old drawing.
3. **Meeting Ria:** ORACLE speaks in Ria's voice. Kael shows the sketchbook, which is the one input ORACLE can't predict.
4. **The Choice:** decide ORACLE's fate. Endings above.

## 8. Recurring motifs
- **The sketchbook / pencil:** analog humanity, Dev's lesson.
- **Hands:** Kael uses his hands; ORACLE has none.
- **"Status: reallocated":** appears at the start and again at the end with a different meaning.
- **Rain and amber light:** quiet sadness.
- **Score:** sparse piano and low synth; the final theme is Ria's melody.

## 9. Open items
- Name check (Varn, Okoro, Reyes, Lind): change freely.
- Voice acting vs. text.
- Exact mission puzzles (to be designed per mission in `docs/missions/`).

## 10. Trust System (per character)

Each ally has a **Trust score (0-100)** and a tier:

| Tier | Range | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Hostile | 0-19 | Refuses help, may sabotage or leave |
| Wary | 20-39 | Does the minimum, hides information |
| Neutral | 40-59 | Cooperates on plans |
| Loyal | 60-79 | Offers extra solutions, shares secrets |
| Bonded | 80-100 | Personal mission unlocked, takes risks for Kael |

### Each ally values different things
| Ally | Trust goes UP when you... | Trust goes DOWN when you... |
|---|---|---|
| **Mira** | Are honest, share intel, protect civilians, help find her parents | Lie, use her without asking, hide what you found |
| **Doc Tane** | Show mercy, risk yourself to save others, don't kill needlessly | Kill when avoidable, sacrifice innocents, brush off his guilt |
| **Jun** | Keep your word, plan carefully, protect the team, don't make him fight his past | Take reckless risks, put the team in danger, force him to hurt people |
| **Sera** | Give her a chance, keep her cover safe, listen to her warnings | Test or expose her, treat her as an enemy, ignore her intel |

### How trust is earned
- **Dialogue choices** during briefings and downtime.
- **Mission choices** (e.g. who does the risky role in the plan).
- **Personal missions** (Bonded tier): each ally has a short side story tied to their wound, with a hard choice.
- **Safe-house scenes** between missions: shared meals, small talk, quiet moments that build bonds.
- Trust is **visible only through behaviour**, never as a number. Allies react in dialogue, body language and reliability.

### What trust unlocks
- New solutions in heists (e.g. high Mira trust opens a back-door hack, Jun lets you use a guard's route).
- Revealed secrets and emotional scenes.
- Survival in the finale.

## 11. Betrayals

Betrayal is **not scripted for one character**. It depends on the player's choices, so any ally can betray, and the story reads differently each time. A betrayal is only possible if the character's trust is low or their secret is poorly handled.

### Why each ally might betray
| Ally | Betrayal trigger | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Mira** | Trust below 30 *and* you hid or lied about her parents | Hands your location to ORACLE in exchange for her father's whereabouts |
| **Doc Tane** | Trust below 30 *and* you dismissed his guilt or let his patient die | Believes ORACLE's care is better, and leaves you to return to the system |
| **Jun** | Trust below 30 *and* you put the team in danger | Returns to enforcement and fights you in the finale |
| **Sera** | Trust below 40 *and* you exposed or tested her | She's already a double agent. Without trust she picks ORACLE's side, because she believes it's the only way to survive |

### Betrayal types
- **Quiet betrayal:** an ally leaks info. The next mission has extra guards, and you piece together who did it.
- **Open betrayal:** an ally confronts you in a mission and chooses a side.
- **Forced betrayal:** ORACLE or Vance blackmails an ally with their secret, so they betray you while hating it. These can be forgiven.
- **The redemption turn:** if Trust is repaired after a forced betrayal, the ally sacrifices something to help you in the finale.

### Foreshadowing (fair play)
Betrayals should be guessable in hindsight:
- Missed calls, nervous glances, small lies in dialogue.
- Objects out of place in the safe house.
- A pointed line in the notebook ("Who knew about the route?").
- The player can **investigate** suspicions and **confront** the ally before the betrayal. Handled well, you can avoid it.

### The betrayal mission
At the end of **Mission 4 or 5**, if any ally is below the threshold, a **betrayal event** fires. It changes the next mission (extra guards, a missing ally, a trap) and shapes the finale and endings.

### What you can do
- **Forgive:** keep them, with reduced trust and tension.
- **Confront:** a hard dialogue scene with different outcomes.
- **Cut them loose:** safer, but you lose their role.
- **Rebuild:** a personal mission to regain trust, if you act early.

---

## 12. Interaction between Trust, Betrayal and Endings

| Situation | Effect on the finale |
|---|---|
| All 4 allies Bonded | Unlocks **Release** (hidden ending) |
| 1 ally betrayed, forgiven | Possible redemption beat in the climax |
| 1 ally betrayed, cut off | That ally's role is lost; ending leans toward Rewrite or Shutdown |
| 2+ betrayals | **Fracture** ending: the team falls apart before Convergence |
| Sera's secret revealed with high Truth *and* Trust | Sera sacrifices to open the core. A major tearjerker |
| Sera's secret revealed with low Trust | She flees or turns on Kael |

### Key emotional payoffs
- **Forgiveness:** Kael, who couldn't trust, forgives someone who hurt him. That's his arc completing.
- **Loss:** an ally Kael pushed away fights against him, and he has to face it.
- **Found family:** the full Bonded team standing together at the core, with Ria's melody playing.

## 13. Implementation notes (for later)
- `TrustManager` holds a score per ally plus flags (secretKnown, lied, forgiven).
- Dialogue and mission choices call `TrustManager.Modify(ally, amount, reason)`.
- A `BetrayalEvaluator` runs at the end of Missions 3, 4 and 5 against the thresholds above.
- Mission scenes check ally status (`Present / Absent / Hostile / Redeemed`) to enable or disable roles.
- Endings are decided by a final `EndingResolver` using Trust, Mercy, Truth, ally status, and Anya/Null/Vance flags.
